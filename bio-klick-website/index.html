FAQ

Quick answers about Bio Klick.

What is Bio Klick?

Bio Klick lets you create a Bio Page that brings your contact details, social media, links and more into one place. You share it with an NFC product, a QR code or a link.

Is Bio Klick free?

Bio Klick is free to start. Download the app and create your Bio Page.

Do I need an NFC card to use it?

No. You can share your Bio Page with a QR code or a link. An NFC card or tag makes sharing faster, with one touch.

How do people open my Bio Page?

They can touch your NFC product, scan your QR code, or open your link.

Can I change my information later?

Yes. Update your phone number, job title, website or social links once, and your latest details show right away.

Can I see how people use my page?

Yes. The app shows your visits, link clicks and CTR, your top links, and who visits by device, system and location.

Where can I get the app?

Bio Klick is on the App Store for iPhone and on Google Play for Android.

Where can I buy NFC products?

The Bio Klick Basic Card is in the Products section. The Funky Card and the Keychain are coming soon.
